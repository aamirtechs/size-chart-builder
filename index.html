Size Chart Builder Builder

Build a clean size chart once and export it as embeddable HTML, a printable PNG image and a Shopify-ready table, in inches and cm, free in your browser.

Free100% in your browserNo sign-up

Runs 100% in your browser. Your files never leave this device.

How it works

  • Editable grid with garment presets: tops, shoes US/UK/EU and kids
  • Automatic inches to cm conversion both ways
  • Fit notes and measurement labels in the chart title
  • Export responsive HTML table, PNG via canvas and CSV
  • Brand colour control for the table header
